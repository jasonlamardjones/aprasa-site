Quick Guide

Four useful starting points.

Choose the situation closest to what you need now: arriving, getting around, living day to day, or staying longer.

01

Arriving

Cesária Évora International Airport is at São Pedro. Taxis serve the airport; confirm the fare before departure. An ATM is reasonably corroborated, but availability and service status can change.

Do not assume SIM sales or foreign-currency exchange are available at the airport. Shared vans on the São Pedro–Mindelo corridor may pass near the airport, but they are not a formal airport shuttle and service is variable.

02

Getting Around

TRANSCOR information is treated as operator-supported route orientation only: A PRASA does not infer official route geometry from stop lists.

For aluguers, Praça Estrela is the orientation hub for the four reviewed destination corridors below.

03

Living Day to Day

Many smaller businesses in Mindelo — local lojas, markets and some supermarkets — close for a midday pause typically around 13:00–15:00. Smaller local shops, markets and supermarkets each work well for different everyday shopping needs, so it is worth knowing more than one option.

Carry some Cape Verde escudos: smaller businesses may not always accept cards. If you are only in Mindelo for a short stay, plan errands around the midday closures rather than assuming everything stays open through the afternoon.

04

Staying Longer

Need to stay longer? Immigration and visa-stay matters are handled through Cabo Verde's Polícia Nacional / Direção de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras. Start the process before your authorized stay expires and confirm the current procedure for your nationality and situation.

A PRASA does not provide immigration or legal advice. The exact current public walk-in immigration counter is not pinned in this guide.
