Quick Guide

Four journeys, with uncertainty kept visible.

01

Arriving

Cesária Évora International Airport is at São Pedro. Taxis serve the airport; confirm the fare before departure. An ATM is reasonably corroborated, but availability and service status can change.

Do not assume SIM sales or foreign-currency exchange are available at the airport. Shared vans on the São Pedro–Mindelo corridor may pass near the airport, but they are not a formal airport shuttle and service is variable.

02

Getting Around

TRANSCOR information is treated as operator-supported route orientation only: A PRASA does not infer official route geometry from stop lists.

For aluguers, Praça Estrela is the orientation hub for the four Release 1 destination corridors below. Vehicles commonly leave when sufficiently full rather than to a published timetable.

03

Living Day to Day

ELECTRA is the utility/customer-service reference for piped water—not a bottled-water supplier. Lavandaria DnD is the bounded laundry provider in this release. Cleber Moreira provides a supported electronics-and-repair option.

For pharmacy needs, Farmácia Jovem is a canonical place record, while after-hours duty pharmacy status is a rotating ERIS assignment and should be checked as a current system rather than assumed from a permanent pin.

04

Staying Longer

Need to stay longer? Immigration and visa-stay matters are handled through Cabo Verde's Polícia Nacional / Direção de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras. Start the process before your authorized stay expires and confirm the current procedure for your nationality and situation.

A PRASA does not provide immigration or legal advice. The exact current public walk-in immigration counter is not pinned in this release.
