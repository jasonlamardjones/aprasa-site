Founding Preview

A PRASA is a shared place where paths, people, and possibilities meet.

A PRASA is an independent platform being built to make useful opportunities and practical local information easier to find and reach.

Think of it as a small digital praça: a place to discover useful starting points and go directly to the people and organizations behind them.

This early edition begins with a carefully limited selection rather than trying to cover everything at once.

A PRASA will grow gradually as information is reviewed, improved and shown to be genuinely useful.

HP Foundation / HP LIFE

HP LIFE: free business and digital-skills courses

HP LIFE offers 32 free online courses in business, digital skills, entrepreneurship, communication, finance, marketing, and professional development. All 32 courses are currently available in eight languages, including Portuguese. Learners who complete a course can receive a free Certificate of Completion.

You must create an account and sign in to enroll. HP LIFE Certificates of Completion are not accredited or endorsed by educational bodies.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

The Open University / OpenLearn

OpenLearn: free courses from The Open University

OpenLearn provides hundreds of free courses across nine subject areas, including digital and computing, languages, money and business, science, and society. All OpenLearn courses are free to study and are available to start now.

The material checked for this record was in English; Portuguese availability has not been confirmed. Successful completion can provide a free Statement of Participation, but OpenLearn free courses do not carry formal credit toward a qualification, and digital badges are available only on some courses.

Instituto do Emprego e Formação Profissional (IEFP)

IEFP PEPE: employment and professional-internship portal

PEPE is IEFP's platform for employment and professional-internship opportunities in Cabo Verde. Visitors can review listings, create a candidate account, and sign in to apply directly through the provider.

An account and sign-in are required to apply. Each listing has its own deadline and conditions. A listing shown on PEPE should not be treated as current without checking its individual details.

Practical ways to contribute

Organizations & Ways to Help

Cruz Vermelha de Cabo Verde

Volunteer with the Red Cross in São Vicente

Volunteer · Donate money

Cruz Vermelha de Cabo Verde accepts volunteer registrations through its national volunteer network and has a local council in São Vicente. Prospective volunteers can register with the provider and coordinate their activities directly with the Red Cross.

Volunteer activities, placement, training and schedules are arranged by Cruz Vermelha de Cabo Verde. The online registration system is national; contact the São Vicente council for local arrangements.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

Register to volunteer ↗

Biosfera

Volunteer with Biosfera in São Vicente

Conservation volunteering · Communication/environmental education · Fundraising

Mindelo-based Biosfera recruits volunteers for conservation and environmental-awareness work in São Vicente and nearby islands. Current routes include turtle-conservation work, communication and environmental education, with fundraising skills also specifically requested.

Turtle-conservation volunteering is seasonal, normally June–October. Roles have different skills and physical requirements; confirm current capacity and arrangements directly with Biosfera before making plans.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

See Biosfera volunteer opportunities ↗

Nô Bai Associação

Volunteer with Nô Bai in São Vicente

Community volunteering

Nô Bai Associação works with communities in São Vicente through activities spanning education, health, the environment and community support. Its international volunteer program accepts applications throughout the year for placements in São Vicente.

Applications involve a form, motivation letter and interview. Seven days is the stated minimum stay, with at least 15 days preferred. Pre-departure training is mandatory; volunteers pay for their own flights and the program has stay-related costs.

Checked 8 August 2026 against information published by the provider.

Volunteer · Donate money · Donate useful goods

Espaço Jovem runs youth centers and activities for children and young people in Mindelo. The organization currently invites people to volunteer, contribute financially, or donate useful materials and food for its programs.
