Independent digital prasa

A PRASA — A place to find what moves you.

Around the world, the town square — a prasa in Cape Verdean Kriolu — is a place where people, paths, and possibilities come together. A PRASA brings the spirit of that place online for communities far and wide.

Come looking for something you need — or discover something new you didn’t know you needed. Find opportunities to grow and learn, immerse yourself in local culture and activities, gain practical local knowledge, discover places worth knowing, and find ways to take part or give back to the local community.

The idea behind the prasa

Why A PRASA exists

Some of the most authentically inspiring — and potentially life-changing — experiences in life don’t begin with a search, but with an encounter: a conversation, a recommendation, a place we pass through, or someone pointing us toward something we didn’t know was there.

Town squares — no matter their shape, form, or location — have always made room for those encounters. They are places where people cross paths, exchange information, celebrate, ask questions, reflect, discover opportunities, help one another, and then continue on their way.

A PRASA exists to intentionally facilitate those useful connections — bringing together practical information, opportunities, insight into local places and culture, and ways to contribute, so you can continue forward on your journey on better footing than when you arrived.

A PRASA points you toward the people and organizations behind the information, so you can check current details and continue from there.
