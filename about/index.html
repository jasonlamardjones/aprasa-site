About A PRASA

Independent, source-led, and built around useful connections.

A PRASA is an independent place for discovering useful information, opportunities, local knowledge, places worth knowing, things to do, and ways to take part or give back.

It began in Cabo Verde, inspired by the familiar role of the public square: a place where people cross paths, exchange information, discover something new, and continue on their way.

About the name

A PRASA draws its name from that shared idea and from Cabo Verde, where the project first took shape.

Prasa is a Kriolu form related to Portuguese praça. Kriolu is a living language, and pronunciation, vocabulary, and writing vary across Cabo Verde’s islands and communities. A PRASA uses PRASA as its chosen brand form; the full name is not presented as a literal or universal Kriolu expression.

The name reflects the larger idea behind the public square: a shared place where people, paths, information, opportunities, and possibilities can meet.

Independent by design

A PRASA is independent. It is not part of, and does not act on behalf of, the organizations, institutions, businesses, public bodies, or other providers referenced or linked through the platform.

Listings and references are provided to help people discover useful possibilities and reach the original source. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, or affiliation unless A PRASA explicitly says otherwise.

A PRASA does not guarantee eligibility, availability, acceptance, accuracy at the time you act, or any particular outcome.

Details can change. Whenever possible, A PRASA points you toward the people and organizations behind the information so you can confirm current details directly before acting.
