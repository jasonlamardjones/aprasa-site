## Technical shell requirements

- Keep one page-level `h1`.
- Render each approved section as a semantically separate section with a stable heading ID suitable for deep links.
- Preserve keyboard-visible focus behavior for in-page navigation.
- Keep the Home About treatment as a concise gateway rather than duplicating the full page.
- Preserve existing site header, footer, responsive container system, analytics behavior, and Identity v2.0 assets unchanged.
- Do not add founder biography, corporate history, manifesto, giant roadmap, or a separate values page in this iteration.
- Do not publish final mission, vision, commercial-sales, Local Spotlight, Sponsored Spotlight, or pricing copy through this shell unless Project 03 supplies and approves that copy.
