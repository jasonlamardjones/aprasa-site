Centro Cultural do Mindelo

CARTINHA D’HOLANDA

Dates
Opens 12 September 2026 · On view through 15 November 2026
Admission
Free admission

Details

The exhibition presents stories of Cape Verdean Rotterdammers through photographs, personal histories, art and heritage.

It opens at Centro Cultural do Mindelo on Saturday, 12 September 2026 at 18:30 and is presented across Centro Cultural do Mindelo and CNAD through 15 November 2026.

Good to know

Admission is free. The partner states that the exhibition is open daily except Sundays, with visiting hours of 10:00–13:00 and 15:00–20:00.

The published exhibition period is subject to change, so confirm current access with the venues before visiting.
