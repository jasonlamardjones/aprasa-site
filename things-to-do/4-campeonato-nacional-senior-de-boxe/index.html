4.º Campeonato Nacional Sénior de Boxe

Date

Where

Provider

Details

The 4.º Campeonato Nacional Sénior de Boxe takes place in São Vicente from 4 to 6 September 2026 at Pavilhão dos Salesianos.

More than 50 senior boxers from across Cabo Verde are expected to participate.

Checked 2 September 2026 against current reporting of the Federação Cabo-verdiana de Boxe announcement.
