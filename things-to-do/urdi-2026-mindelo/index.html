URDI 2026 — Cabo Verde Crafts & Design Fair

Dates
25–29 November 2026
Venue
Praça Amílcar Cabral (Praça Nova), Mindelo, São Vicente
Organizer
Centro Nacional de Arte, Artesanato e Design (CNAD)

Details

Organized by CNAD, this year's fair celebrates 50 years of memory, creation and resistance, with artisan exhibitors and accompanying cultural activities.

Good to know

The fair takes place in a public square. Check CNAD's official information for the current programme and any activity-specific access requirements.
