Mindelo Cultural

Learn Cape Verdean Dance in Mindelo

When
17:00 daily except Sunday · booking and availability apply
Where
Starts at Palácio do Povo, Mindelo
Price
€60

Details

Join a 1.5-hour workshop exploring six Cape Verdean rhythms with live music. Mindelo Cultural offers the participatory dance experience for visitors who want a more direct introduction to local music and dance traditions.

Good to know

Advance booking and availability apply. Mindelo Cultural states that bookings should be made 24 hours in advance.
