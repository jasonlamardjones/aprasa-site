TXON KUCEU

VOYAGE — OBI & MARGO

Dates
16–30 September 2026
Venue
Kafe Djan Djan
Program
16, 23 and 30 September · 18:00–20:00
Admission
Free and open to the public

Details

VOYAGE is an exhibition by OBI & MARGO, visual artists and hand-poke tattoo specialists from Croatia and France. Their journey in Cabo Verde became a source of artistic exploration shaped by new encounters, landscapes and the experience of discovering a sense of home. The exhibition runs at Kafe Djan Djan from 16–30 September 2026. The programme includes “Vitoria pa frente, tristeza pa trás,” an opening and autobiographical performance with puppets, headlamps and kazoo on 16 September; “The Transformation,” an interactive hand-poke tattoo ceremony on 23 September; and “Remember to follow the Sun,” a playful meditation session and exhibition closing on 30 September. All three events are stated by the organizer’s poster to be free and open to the public.

Good to know

The organizer states that all listed events are free and open to the public. The 18:00–20:00 time applies to the three listed program events and should not be presented as the exhibition’s daily opening hours.
